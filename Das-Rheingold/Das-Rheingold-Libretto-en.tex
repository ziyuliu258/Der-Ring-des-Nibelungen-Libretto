\begin{center}\small\color{stage}\emph{Woglinde, Wellgunde, Flosshilde, Alberich}\end{center}
\begin{stageblock}
\end{stageblock}
\stage{At the bottom of the Rhine.}
\stage{Greenish twilight, lighter above, darker below. The upper reaches are filled with surging water that streams restlessly from right to left. Towards the depths the floods dissolve into an ever finer damp mist, so that the space to a man's height above the ground seems entirely free of the water, which flows like banks of cloud over the night-dark bed. Everywhere steep rocky reefs jut up from the depths and bound the space of the stage; the whole ground is split into a wild tangle of jagged points, so that nowhere is it quite level, and on every side it falls away in deepest darkness into lower chasms.}
\stage{Around a reef in the middle of the stage, whose slender peak rises up into the denser, more brightly glimmering water, one of the Rhinemaidens circles in graceful swimming motion.}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Weia! Waga! Surge, you wave,}
\vzeile{roll to the cradle! Wagalaweia!}
\vzeile{Wallala, weiala weia!}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\stagedir{voice from above}
\vzeile{Woglinde, do you keep watch alone?}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{With Wellgunde I would be two.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\stagedir{dives down from the flood to the reef}
\vzeile{Let me see how you keep watch!}
\stagedir{she tries to catch Woglinde}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\stagedir{eludes her, swimming}
\vzeile{Safe from you!}
\stagedir{They tease one another and try playfully to catch each other}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{voice from above}
\vzeile{Heiaha weia! Wild sisters!}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Flosshilde, swim! Woglinde is fleeing:}
\vzeile{help me catch the one who slips away!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{dives down and darts between the two at play}
\vzeile{Badly you guard the sleeping gold!}
\vzeile{Keep better watch over the slumberer's bed,}
\vzeile{or you will both pay for your sport!}
\stagedir{With merry shrieks the two dart apart. Flosshilde tries to catch first one, then the other; they slip away from her and at last join together to hunt Flosshilde in their turn. So they dart like fish from reef to reef, jesting and laughing.}
\stagedir{Meanwhile Alberich has climbed out of the abyss from a dark chasm, clambering up a reef. Still wrapped in darkness, he stops and watches the Rhinemaidens' play with growing delight.}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Hehe! You nixies!}
\vzeile{How dainty you are, enviable folk!}
\vzeile{Gladly would I come up from Nibelheim's night,}
\vzeile{if you would bend down to me!}
\stagedir{As soon as they hear Alberich's voice, the maidens stop their play}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Hei! Who is there?}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{It grows dark, and someone calls!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Look who is spying on us!}
\speaker{Woglinde and Wellgunde}
\stagedir{they dive deeper and recognise the Nibelung}
\vzeile{Pfui! The ugly one!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{darting quickly up}
\vzeile{Guard the gold!}
\vzeile{Father warned us of such a foe.}
\stagedir{The other two follow her, and all three gather quickly around the middle reef}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{You, up there!}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{What do you want down there?}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Do I spoil your play,}
\vzeile{if I stand here marvelling in silence?}
\vzeile{If you dived down, the Nibelung would gladly}
\vzeile{romp and tease with you!}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{He wants to play with us?}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Is he mocking?}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{How bright and fair you seem in the shimmer!}
\vzeile{How gladly my arm would clasp one of the slender ones,}
\vzeile{if she glided kindly down!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Now I laugh at my fear: the foe is in love!}
\stagedir{They laugh}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{The lecherous fellow!}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Let us get to know him!}
\stagedir{She lets herself down onto the peak of the reef, at whose foot Alberich has arrived.}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{She bends down to me.}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Now come close to me!}
\stagedir{Alberich climbs towards the peak of the reef with impish agility, but is repeatedly held back}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Nasty, smooth, slippery glimmer!}
\vzeile{How I keep sliding! With hands and feet}
\vzeile{I can neither grip nor hold the sleek slither!}
\stagedir{he snorts}
\vzeile{Wet damp fills my nose:}
\vzeile{accursed sneezing!}
\stagedir{he has come close to Woglinde}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\stagedir{laughing}
\vzeile{Snorting, my suitor's splendour draws near!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Be my sweetheart, you maidenly child!}
\stagedir{he tries to embrace her}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\stagedir{slipping free of him}
\vzeile{If you would woo me, then woo me here!}
\stagedir{she surfaces at another reef; the sisters laugh}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{scratches his head}
\vzeile{Alas! You escape? Come back!}
\vzeile{What you swing up so lightly costs me dear.}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\stagedir{swings herself onto a third reef, deeper down}
\vzeile{Just climb to the bottom, there you will catch me for sure!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{clambering hastily down}
\vzeile{Better down below, then!}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\stagedir{darts quickly upwards to a high reef at the side}
\vzeile{But now up above!}
\speaker{Wellgunde and Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Hahahahaha!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{How shall I catch the coy fish as she leaps?}
\vzeile{Wait, you false one!}
\stagedir{he tries to climb hastily after her}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\stagedir{has sunk down to a lower reef on the other side}
\vzeile{Heia, you handsome one! Do you not hear me?}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{turning round}
\vzeile{Are you calling me?}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{I counsel you well: turn to me,}
\vzeile{leave Woglinde be!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{clambers hastily over the river bed towards Wellgunde}
\vzeile{You are far fairer than that shy one,}
\vzeile{who gleams less brightly and is all too sleek.}
\vzeile{Only dive deeper, if you would suit me.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\stagedir{letting herself sink a little closer to him}
\vzeile{Am I near you now?}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Not near enough!}
\vzeile{Wind your slender arms about me,}
\vzeile{so that I may teasingly stroke your neck,}
\vzeile{and with coaxing ardour}
\vzeile{nestle against your swelling breast.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{If you are in love and lusting for love,}
\vzeile{let me see, you handsome one, what you look like!}
\vzeile{Pfui! You hairy, humpbacked fop!}
\vzeile{Black, calloused sulphur-dwarf!}
\vzeile{Find yourself a sweetheart whom you please!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{tries to hold her by force}
\vzeile{If I do not please you, I will hold you fast all the same!}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\stagedir{darting quickly up to the middle reef}
\vzeile{Hold fast, or I flow away from you!}
\speaker{Woglinde and Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Hahahahaha!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{scolding angrily after Wellgunde}
\vzeile{False child! Cold, bony fish!}
\vzeile{If I do not seem fair to you,}
\vzeile{dainty and playful, sleek and smooth --}
\vzeile{hei, then go courting eels, if my hide disgusts you!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Why scold, elf? So downcast already?}
\vzeile{You have wooed two: ask the third,}
\vzeile{and the beloved would bring you sweet comfort!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Lovely song comes singing to me!}
\vzeile{How good that you are not but one!}
\vzeile{Of many, one at least might like me:}
\vzeile{with only one, none would choose me!}
\vzeile{If I am to believe you, glide down here!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{dives down to Alberich}
\vzeile{How foolish you are, stupid sisters,}
\vzeile{not to think him fair!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{approaching her}
\vzeile{I may well think them stupid and ugly,}
\vzeile{now that I see you, loveliest one.}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{coaxingly}
\vzeile{O sing on, so sweet and fine;}
\vzeile{how nobly it beguiles my ear!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{touching her confidingly}
\vzeile{My heart falters, flutters and wastes away,}
\vzeile{when such dainty praise smiles on me.}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{gently fending him off}
\vzeile{How your grace delights my eye,}
\vzeile{how the mildness of your smile refreshes my courage!}
\stagedir{She draws him blissfully to her}
\vzeile{Most blessed of men!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Sweetest of maids!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{If only you were fond of me!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{If I held you for ever!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{holding him wholly in her arms}
\vzeile{Your piercing glance, your bristly beard --}
\vzeile{o, might I see it, might I grasp it always!}
\vzeile{The stiff locks of your prickly hair --}
\vzeile{might they flow about Flosshilde for ever!}
\vzeile{Your toad-like shape, the croak of your voice --}
\vzeile{o, might I, marvelling and dumb,}
\vzeile{only hear and see them!}
\speaker{Woglinde and Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Hahahahaha!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{starting up in fright from Flosshilde's arms}
\vzeile{Are you wicked ones laughing at me?}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\stagedir{suddenly tearing herself from him}
\vzeile{As is only fitting at the end of the song!}
\stagedir{she darts quickly up with her sisters}
\speaker{Woglinde and Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Hahahahaha!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{in a shrieking voice}
\vzeile{Woe! Ah, woe! O pain! O pain!}
\vzeile{The third, so dear -- has she betrayed me too?}
\vzeile{You shamefully cunning, wanton, worthless rabble!}
\vzeile{Do you live on nothing but deceit, you faithless brood of nixies?}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{Wallala! Lalaleia! Leialalei!}
\vzeile{Heia! Heia! Haha!}
\vzeile{Shame on you, elf! Do not scold down there!}
\vzeile{Hear what we bid you!}
\vzeile{Why, faint-heart, did you not hold fast}
\vzeile{the maiden you love?}
\vzeile{We are true, and without deceit,}
\vzeile{to the suitor who catches us.}
\vzeile{Just seize hold, and do not be afraid!}
\vzeile{In the flood we do not easily escape!}
\vzeile{Wallala! Lalaleia! Leialalei!}
\vzeile{Heia! Heia! Haha!}
\stagedir{They swim apart, hither and thither, now deeper, now higher, to entice Alberich into hunting them.}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{How a lustful fire burns and glows}
\vzeile{in my limbs!}
\vzeile{Rage and love, wild and mighty,}
\vzeile{stir up my courage!}
\vzeile{Laugh and lie as you will,}
\vzeile{I lust and thirst for you,}
\vzeile{and one of you must yield to me!}
\stagedir{With desperate effort he sets off in pursuit: with terrible agility he climbs reef after reef, leaps from one to another, and tries to catch now this maiden, now that, while they always slip away from him with merry shrieks. He stumbles, plunges down into the abyss, then clambers hastily up again to renew the chase. They bend down a little. He almost reaches them, falls back once more, and tries again. At last, foaming with rage, he stops, breathless, and stretches his clenched fist up towards the maidens.}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{scarcely master of himself}
\vzeile{If only this fist could catch one!...}
\stagedir{He remains in speechless fury, his gaze turned upward, where he is then suddenly caught and held fast by the spectacle that follows.}
\stagedir{From above, an ever brighter gleam has pierced the flood, and gradually kindles at a high point of the middle reef into a blindingly radiant golden glow: a magical golden light breaks from there through the water.}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Look, sisters!}
\vzeile{The wakener laughs down into the depths.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Through the green swell}
\vzeile{she greets the blissful sleeper.}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Now she kisses his eye, so that he opens it.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{See, he smiles in the bright gleam.}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Through the waters flows his radiant star!}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\stagedir{swimming gracefully together around the reef}
\vzeile{Heiajaheia! Heiajaheia!}
\vzeile{Wallalalalala leiajahei!}
\vzeile{Rhinegold! Rhinegold!}
\vzeile{Radiant joy, how bright and noble you laugh!}
\vzeile{Glowing splendour streams solemnly from you through the waves!}
\vzeile{Heiajaheia! Heiajaheia!}
\vzeile{Wake, friend, wake in joy!}
\vzeile{Blissful games we offer you:}
\vzeile{the river shimmers, the flood flames,}
\vzeile{and diving, dancing and singing we flow about you,}
\vzeile{your bed, in the blessed bath!}
\vzeile{Rhinegold! Rhinegold!}
\vzeile{Heiajaheia! Wallalalalala leiajahei!}
\stagedir{With ever wilder delight the maidens swim around the reef. The whole flood shimmers in a bright golden glow.}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{his eyes, powerfully drawn by the gleam, fixed staring on the gold}
\vzeile{What is it, you sleek ones, that glitters and gleams there?}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{Where do you come from, rough one,}
\vzeile{that you have never heard of the Rhinegold?}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Does the elf know nothing of the gold's eye,}
\vzeile{that wakes and sleeps by turns?}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Of the blissful star of the water's depths,}
\vzeile{that nobly lights the waves through?}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{See how blissfully we glide in its radiance!}
\vzeile{If you, faint-heart, would bathe in it,}
\vzeile{then swim and revel with us!}
\vzeile{Wallalalala leialalai! Wallalalala leiajahei!}
\speaker{Alberich}
\vzeile{Is the gold good for nothing but your diving games?}
\vzeile{Then it would be worth little to me!}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{He would not scorn the gold's adornment,}
\vzeile{if he knew all its wonders!}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{The inheritance of the world would be won}
\vzeile{by him who forged from the Rhinegold the ring}

\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Our father told us so, and bade us}

\vzeile{so that no false one might steal it from the flood:}
\vzeile{so be silent, you chattering brood!}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Wisest sister, do you rebuke us?}
\vzeile{Do you not know, then, to whom alone}
\vzeile{it is granted to forge the gold?}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Only he who renounces the power of love,}
\vzeile{only he who drives away the joy of love,}
\vzeile{only he wins for himself the magic}
\vzeile{to compel the gold into a ring.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Safe indeed we are, and free of care:}
\vzeile{for all that lives will love,}
\vzeile{and none will shun love.}
\speaker{Woglinde}
\vzeile{Least of all he, the lustful elf;}
\vzeile{he would perish for greed of love!}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{I do not fear him, such as I found him:}
\vzeile{the ardour of his love all but burned me.}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{A sulphur-brand in the swell of the waves:}
\vzeile{he hisses loud with the fury of love!}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{Wallala! Wallaleialala!}
\vzeile{Loveliest elf! Will you not laugh too?}
\vzeile{How fair you shine in the gleam of the gold!}
\vzeile{O come, lovely one, laugh with us!}
\vzeile{Heiajaheia! Heiajaheia! Wallalalala leiajahei!}
\stagedir{They swim up and down in the radiance, laughing.}

\stagedir{his eyes fixed staring on the gold, has listened well to the sisters' chatter}

\vzeile{If I cannot compel love,}
\vzeile{might I yet cunningly compel delight for myself?}
\stagedir{terribly loud}
\vzeile{Mock on, then! --}
\vzeile{The Nibelung draws near your play!}
\stagedir{in a fury he leaps across to the middle reef and climbs with hideous haste towards its peak. The maidens dart apart shrieking and dive up on different sides}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{Heia! Heia! Heiajahei!}
\vzeile{Save yourselves! The elf is raving:}
\vzeile{the water sprays wherever he leaps:}
\vzeile{love has driven him mad!}
\stagedir{they laugh in the wildest high spirits}
\speaker{Alberich}
\stagedir{reaches the peak of the reef with a final bound}
\vzeile{Are you not afraid yet?}
\vzeile{Then go courting in the dark, you watery brood!}
\stagedir{he stretches his hand out towards the gold}
\vzeile{I quench your light, I tear the gold from the reef,}
\vzeile{I forge the avenging ring;}
\vzeile{for hear it, flood: thus I curse love!}
\stagedir{With terrible force he tears the gold from the reef and plunges hastily with it into the depths, where he quickly disappears. Dense night falls suddenly everywhere. The maidens dive down after the robber into the depths.}
\speaker{Flosshilde}
\vzeile{Stop the robber!}
\speaker{Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Save the gold!}
\speaker{Woglinde and Wellgunde}
\vzeile{Help! Help!}
\speaker{The Three Rhinemaidens}
\vzeile{Woe! Woe!}
\stagedir{The flood sinks down with them into the depths. From the lowest ground Alberich's shrill mocking laughter is heard. The reefs vanish in deepest darkness; the whole stage, from top to bottom, is filled with black waves of water, which for a time still seem to sink downwards.}
\chapter*{Scene Two}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Zweite Szene}
\markright{Zweite Szene}
\begin{center}\small\color{stage}\emph{Wotan, Fricka, Freia, Fasolt, Fafner, Donner, Froh, Loge}\end{center}
\begin{stageblock}
\end{stageblock}
\stage{Little by little the waves have turned to banks of cloud, which, as an ever brighter glimmering light rises behind them, clear into a finer mist. When the mist has quite dissolved overhead into delicate little clouds, an open space on the mountain heights becomes visible in the grey of dawn.}
\stage{The breaking day lights with growing radiance a castle with glittering battlements, standing on a rocky summit in the background; between this castle-crowned peak and the foreground of the scene a deep valley is to be imagined, through which the Rhine flows. -- To one side, on a flowery bank, lies Wotan, and beside him Fricka, both asleep. The castle has become fully visible.}
\speaker{Fricka}
\stagedir{wakes; her gaze falls on the castle; she is astonished and alarmed}
\vzeile{Wotan, husband, awake!}
\speaker{Wotan}
\stagedir{softly, in his dream}
\vzeile{The blessed hall of delight --}
\vzeile{gate and door guard it for me:}
\vzeile{man's honour, eternal power,}
